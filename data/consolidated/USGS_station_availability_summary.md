## Reading the table

- **Not cataloged** means no matching precipitation/discharge/level series was found in this saved USGS catalog; it does not establish that no data exist elsewhere. Other parameters, such as water chemistry, are outside these five columns.
- **Subdaily** means an instantaneous/unit-value series is cataloged. Catalog presence and record end dates alone do not prove current operation. Date ranges are the earliest start/latest end across matching series of the indicated type; they do not imply continuous coverage.
- **Spot/sample observations** are intermittent measurements associated with sampling, not continuous telemetry. Discharge includes parameter 00061 (instantaneous discharge associated with samples) as well as continuous discharge parameter 00060.
- Groundwater is labeled explicitly; it is not reservoir stage. Surface-water stage/elevation and groundwater values can have different vertical datums.
- *Annual-report catalog entries are pointers to annual documentation, not verified stage time series. No stage values or coverage were inferred from them.
- **Buffer only** means outside that reservoir’s watershed but within 2 km of its boundary. Distance is to the watershed boundary, not to the reservoir or station outlet. Hydraulic connectivity is not established.
